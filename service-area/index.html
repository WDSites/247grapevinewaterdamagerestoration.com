What changes from town to town

The service list is the same everywhere, but the questions are not. Near Grapevine Lake and Denton Creek the first question is flood coverage. In towns that grew quickly from the 1980s through the 2000s, such as Southlake and Coppell, aging water heaters, supply lines and roofs lead the calls. In Euless, irrigation lines and winter freezes are the seasonal pattern.

Utilities differ too. Grapevine runs its own Water and Wastewater Division, Colleyville's Public Works runs both systems, Coppell buys water from Dallas Water Utilities and Flower Mound receives water from both the Upper Trinity Regional Water District and Dallas Water Utilities. If water is surfacing outside the house, the utility is the first call, and we handle the damage inside.
