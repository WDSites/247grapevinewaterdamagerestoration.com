What to tell the dispatcher

A few facts help us plan the right crew and equipment.

Where is the water from?

A pipe, an appliance, the roof, a drain or outdoors. If you are not sure, say so.

What did it touch?

Rooms, floors, walls, ceilings and contents that are wet.

Is it still running?

Whether the valve is shut off and whether water is still spreading.
