Which service do you need?

Water damage work happens in a sequence. Water is stopped and removed first, the structure is dried, contaminated or ruined materials come out and the rebuild comes last. Each page below covers one stage, so you can read the part that matches where you are.

If water is still moving, start with emergency water removal. If it has stopped but the rooms are soaked, mitigation and cleanup come next. If everything is dry and the damage remains, repair is the right page. For the main overview of water damage restoration in Grapevine, see the homepage.

How the standards shape each service

Mitigation, cleanup and drying follow the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration, and mold remediation follows S520. Both are published industry standards, and you are welcome to ask any company how its crews apply them.

How we price and explain the work

Every job is different, so there is no list price on this site. The factors that set cost are the water category, the affected area, the materials involved, the number of drying days and the rebuild scope. Scope and price are explained before work starts, and you can ask what each line includes.

Insurance policies differ, too. Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered and gradual leaks and flooding often are not, so check your policy or ask your insurer. We document the loss with photos and moisture readings and make no promise about how a claim will be decided.
