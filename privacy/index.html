Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you call us or visit this website.

What this site collects

This website has no forms, accounts or checkout. We do not ask you to submit personal details on the site. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as the pages requested and the time of the request.

Phone calls

Our service is call-only. When you phone the emergency line, we ask for your name, address and a description of the damage so a crew can be arranged. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How information is used

Information you give on a call is used to arrange service, to follow up on your request and to improve how we answer calls. We share what is needed with the crews that handle your job.

Cookies and third parties

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and embeds Google Maps. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address under their own policies. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you or ask us to delete it by emailing contact@247grapevinewaterdamagerestoration.com. Questions about this policy go to the same address.
