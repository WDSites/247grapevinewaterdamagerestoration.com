If Water Is Still Spreading: what to do before a crew arrives

Do only what is safe.

Shut off the water

Use the fixture valve, the appliance valve or the main. If you are not sure where it is, the dispatcher can help.

Electrical caution

If outlets, light fixtures or the panel are wet, stay out and call the electric utility or 911 before you call us.

Lift and move

Put electronics, paper and small items on counters or beds, and roll wet rugs up if you can do so safely.

Keep clear of dirty water

Water from drains, toilets and outdoors is contaminated, so keep children and pets away.

Record the damage

Video the room and each wet item before you move it, for your insurer.
