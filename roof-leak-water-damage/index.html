How a roof leak reaches your ceiling in Grapevine

Roof leak water damage in Grapevine, TX usually starts at a weak point such as a lifted or cracked shingle, worn flashing around a chimney or vent pipe, a skylight curb or a damaged ridge cap. Wind-driven rain and hail often open these spots. Water then follows gravity into the attic.

It rarely drops straight down. Water runs along the roof deck, down a rafter or across the top of a duct before it soaks insulation and the drywall beneath. A hallway stain can sit a long way from the shingle that let the rain in, which is why the entry point is hard to guess.

Our crew dries the structure and a roofer repairs the roof. We do not patch shingles or flashing. A roofer should find the entry point so the drying does not start over with the next rain.

First-hour safety when rain is coming through

Rain may still be entering, so the aim is to keep people safe and limit what gets wet.

  • If a ceiling fixture, fan or outlet is wet or dripping, stay out of the room and call the electric utility or 911 before you call us. Cut power only from a dry panel you can reach safely.
  • Stay out from under a bulging ceiling, since drywall full of water can drop without warning.
  • Set buckets under drips and carry furniture, rugs and electronics out of the room.
  • Do not climb onto a wet roof. Call a roofer to cover the opening.
  • Photograph and video each stain, drip and damaged item before anything is moved.

How we dry the attic and the ceiling below

Crews follow the ANSI/IICRC S500 reference standard for water damage work, and the steps shift with how far the water traveled.

  1. Map wet areas with moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera from the room below, then confirm what is above.
  2. Pull saturated attic insulation, which presses water onto the ceiling and dries slowly in a closed attic.
  3. Open or remove soft or bulging drywall so trapped water drains and the cavity can dry.
  4. Extract water from carpet and floors under the leak with extraction units.
  5. Place air movers and LGR dehumidifiers, with drying mats on hard floors the water reached.
  6. Return to re-read moisture each day and stop only when the materials test dry.

When an attic is tight or the debris is heavy, containment barriers and HEPA air scrubbers keep dust out of the living space.

Mistakes to avoid after a roof leak

Painting over a ceiling stain hides moisture that is still inside. Another is waiting for a dry day to call, when insulation keeps pressing water into the drywall in the meantime. Skipping the attic check is a third, because the worst of it is often above the ceiling.

Many people throw out wet items too early. Keep them until your insurer has seen them. Have a roofer inspect flashing and shingles after hard storms.
